  A NOTE OF THANKS
  ----------------

  If Volume I was the beginning of the story, Volume II is where things
  get gloriously messy. This is the half of the collection where Manic
  Miner meets Tetris, where Sabre Wulf brushes shoulders with SimCity,
  where Rick Dangerous narrowly avoids Robocop. It is a reminder that the
  ZX Spectrum's library was never one thing — it was arcade ports and epic
  adventures, football sims and surreal art games, all squeezed into 48K
  or 128K by people who refused to accept limitations.

  The musicians, artists, and programmers behind these titles worked in an
  era without the internet, without Stack Overflow, without version control.
  They had the manual, a cup of tea, and sheer determination. The results
  speak for themselves — and they're all here, ready to play.

  This is Volume II. Pick up where you left off, or dive in anywhere.
  There isn't a bad choice in the list.

  And once again, deep thanks to the people who made this distribution
  possible:

    akeley       — for the curated top-100 collection that forms the heart
                   of this release.
                   https://archive.org/details/zxspectrum-top-100

    Bob Fossil   — for tireless documentation and passion for the platform.
                   http://www.thefossilrecord.co.uk/coding/100-machine-code/

    Miguel Guerreiro & Mario Prato
                 — creators of esxDOS, the firmware that makes DivMMC
                   such a joy to use.
                   https://www.esxdos.org/index.html

    Cesar Hernandez Bano
                 — creator of ZEsarUX, an emulator built with love and
                   extraordinary depth.
                   https://github.com/chernandezba/zesarux

    Steve Wetherill
                 — who worked on Nodes of Yesod (game 182), Robin of the
                   Wood (game 213), and Projectyle at Odin, having cut his
                   teeth at Software Projects porting Manic Miner and Jet
                   Set Willy to the Amstrad CPC. A career in games that
                   started there and never stopped.
                   https://stevewetherill.substack.com/p/chapter-7-the-astronaut-and-the-sleeping

  Thank you all for keeping the Spectrum alive and powerful.
